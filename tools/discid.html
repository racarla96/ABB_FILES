ABB S4 DISC.ID tool

Decode, check, generate and re-serialise disc.id files (BaseWare/RobotWare 2.x, 3.0). Everything runs in your browser; no files are uploaded.

The header is kept as is (program, label, make date); only the Serial no line and the hidden binary tail change. Downloaded files keep the name disc.id; save each one into its own disk folder.


  


  
Algorithm: tail of 193 bytes from EOF; serial[i] = (byte[pos_i] + 0xA7 − 2i) & 0xFF, positions 0,18,35,51,… (step 18−i), terminated by 0. Not tested on a real controller; use only with your own equipment and disks.
